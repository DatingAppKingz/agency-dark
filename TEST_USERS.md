## How to Use

1. Navigate to http://localhost:3000
2. Use any of the above credentials to log in
3. Each role has different permissions and access levels in the system

## Role Descriptions

- **Agency Admin**: Has full control over their agency's data and settings
- **Super Admin**: System-wide administrator with access to all agencies
- **Model**: Content creator account with access to their own profile and content
- **Chatter**: Support account for managing conversations and fan interactions
